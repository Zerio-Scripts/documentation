# Description

Zerio Radio v3 is a radio system for FiveM roleplay servers. Players join frequencies, talk with push-to-talk, and deal with signal coverage, jammers, batteries and other radio realism. Radio audio runs on Zerio's hosted voice server (zerio-voice), not through pma-voice or Mumble, so it works next to whatever proximity voice your server already uses.

## Key Features

### Radio

- **Frequencies**: Join any frequency, or only the ones you have access to. Listen to several channels at once and pick which one you transmit on.
- **Push-to-talk**: Its own keybind (Left Alt by default), with transmit and receive sounds that you can change per channel.
- **No-talk-over channels**: Only one person can transmit at a time on channels where you turn this on.
- **Member list**: See who is on the channel and who is talking, with callsigns and an optional anonymous mode.
- **Favorites and recents**: Save frequencies and jump back to recently used ones.
- **Private channels**: Players can create temporary, password-protected channels.
- **Messages and contacts**: Send text messages to other players from the radio.
- **Panic button**: Sends an alert with a sound, screen effect and notification, and passes it on to dispatch.
- **Radio scanner**: Scan for nearby active channels and lock on to listen in.
- **Player settings**: Themes, light and dark mode, three radio skins, and movable, resizable overlays.

### Signal and Realism

- **Radio towers**: Coverage comes from towers you place. Towers can be shot down and repaired in-game.
- **Repeaters**: Players place repeaters to extend tower coverage.
- **Jammers**: Ground jammers and personal (backpack) jammers that kill signal in an area.
- **Signal zones**: Draw zones in the admin menu that force a signal level, from none (tunnels, bunkers) to full.
- **Underground and weather**: Signal drops underground and in bad weather.
- **Audio degradation**: Weak signal sounds worse and breaks up, both for the listener and the transmitter.
- **Battery**: Optional battery drain, battery swaps and charging stations.
- **Ambient capture**: Nearby gunshots and aircraft can be heard in transmissions.
- **Audio leak**: Players standing next to you hear your radio unless you wear an earpiece.

### Coordination

- **Man-down alerts**: Warns, then alerts the channel when a player stays down. Turned on per channel.
- **Unit GPS**: Live map blips for players on the same channel. Turned on per channel.
- **Channel patches**: Link channels so they hear each other.
- **Speakers**: PA speakers placed in the world.
- **Signal 100**: Dispatch can reserve a channel for emergency traffic, so only dispatch can talk on it.
- **Dispatch integrations**: Panic and man-down alerts go to the dispatch console and to zerio-dispatch, ps-dispatch or lb-tablet. Other scripts can hook in through [exports and events](/radio/devdocs).

### Items and Access

- **Radio as an item**: Players need the item to use the radio. Dropping or losing it disconnects them.
- **Codeplug radios**: Items like a police radio that only work on a fixed set of channels, and are the only way onto those channels.
- **Access rules**: Restrict channels by job, gang (QBCore, rcore_gangs, op-crime), ace permission, identifier or Discord role.

### Administration

- **In-game admin menu** (`/radioadmin`): Manage channels, players, towers, repeaters, jammers, signal zones, speakers and patches, and view a signal map.
- **Customer portal**: Set up and manage your voice server at [portal.radio.zerio-scripts.com](https://portal.radio.zerio-scripts.com). The portal also has a web dispatch panel.

## Requirements

### Essential

- **Database**: oxmysql. Other SQL resources (mysql-async, ghmattimysql) are not supported.
- **ox_lib**: Used for keybinds, notifications, interaction prompts and callbacks.
- **zerio-radio-prop**: Streams the radio, repeater and vehicle radio props. It is included in the download and runs as its own resource.
- **OneSync**: Must be enabled.
- **Zerio voice server**: Hosted by Zerio. You set it up from the link in your purchase email.
- **Framework**: QBCore, Qbox, ESX, or none (standalone).

### Optional Dependencies

- **ox_target or qb-target**: Only if you switch jammer, repeater, tower repair or charging station interactions to `"target"`.
- **zdiscord or Badger_Discord_API**: For channels restricted by Discord role.
- **rcore_gangs or op-crime**: For channels restricted to those gang systems.
- **zerio-dispatch, ps-dispatch or lb-tablet**: Panic and man-down alerts can also be sent to one of these.

## Compatibility

### Frameworks

- **QBCore** and **Qbox**: Detected automatically. Qbox runs through its QBCore compatibility layer.
- **ESX**: Detected automatically.
- **Standalone**: Used when no framework is found. There are no items; players open the radio with a command or keybind, and admin features use ace permissions.

### Inventories

- **ox_inventory**: Version 2.47.0 or newer.
- **qb-inventory**: Through QBCore's item functions.
- **ESX inventory**: ESX's built-in inventory.

### Voice

- zerio-radio does not replace your proximity voice. pma-voice, or any other proximity voice resource, keeps handling normal voice chat.
- zerio-radio turns off pma-voice's own radio, radio UI and radio animation on start, so they don't overlap.

### Hardened Servers

zerio-radio works with every setting of `sv_entityLockdown`, `sv_filterRequestControl` and `sv_stateBagStrictMode true`.
